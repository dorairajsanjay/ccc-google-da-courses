Monthly Update

AI & Data Analytics Initiative: Monthly Update

Each month the Data Analytics Initiative team shares its progress, opportunities, and what comes next.

November 2026

Progress

  • Four regional convenings held: San Diego & Imperial (Sep 29), Inland Empire/Desert (Oct 2), North/Far North (Oct 9), and Orange County (Oct 30)
  • Course packages v3.1 released, and all 19 courses can now be downloaded directly
  • This site relaunched as the home for industry certificate pathways

Opportunities

  • Colleges can award Credit for Prior Learning to students who already hold a Google Data Analytics certificate
  • Noncredit cohorts can be funded through the Strong Workforce Program
  • Every course is CC BY 4.0, so colleges can adopt them with no licensing cost

On the Horizon

  • Central Valley (Nov 20), Los Angeles (Dec 1), and Bay Area (Dec 4) convenings
  • Follow-up resources and a survey for all convening attendees
  • South Central Coast convening being scheduled
  • Faculty focus groups for the next certificate pathways

Making it last: the initiative is working toward Academic Senate–endorsed model curriculum, openly licensed course content for all 116 colleges, CPL pathways in the systemwide CPL framework, Strong Workforce funding for ongoing cohorts, and renewed industry agreements that keep content available.

For faculty and deans

Free, ready-to-teach Canvas courses are now available for the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate, built by California Community College faculty. They include 1-unit micro-credentials, 3-unit semester courses, and Credit for Prior Learning versions. All 19 courses are CC BY 4.0 and import into Canvas in minutes: https://dorairajsanjay.github.io/ccc-google-da-courses/courses.html

For students

Earn college credit and a Google Data Analytics certificate at the same time, at no cost. Already have the certificate? You may qualify for Credit for Prior Learning. Ask your counselor or CIS/business department about data analytics courses.
