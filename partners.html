The Model

How New Certificates Are Added

Every certificate goes through the same process, so colleges get a consistent experience no matter which partner the content comes from.

  1. Faculty focus group

    Discipline faculty review the content and choose how to package it: credit, noncredit, embedded, or CPL.

  2. Course design

    Faculty map certificate modules to CCC course outlines and build Canvas shells and assessments.

  3. Open publication

    Courses are released under CC BY 4.0 with instructor guides and placeholders for each college's details.

  4. Statewide kickoff

    The pathway is introduced to faculty and leaders across the system.

  5. Regional convenings

    Colleges plan local adoption with their regional consortium.

  6. Showcase & iterate

    Outcomes are shared and courses are revised from faculty feedback.

Principles

What Stays Constant

Faculty own the curriculum

Partners supply content. Course outlines, assessments, and credit decisions stay with CCC faculty and local curriculum committees.

Free and open

No licensing fees for colleges or students. Course materials are openly licensed so colleges can adapt them.

Credit that counts

Pathways stack toward certificates and degrees. CPL means students are not asked to repeat learning they have already shown.
