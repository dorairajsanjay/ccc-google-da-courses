About the Initiative

It is a Chancellor's Office effort, led by faculty, to turn industry-recognized certificates into California Community College credit. Industry partners such as Google supply certificate content at no cost. CCC faculty design Canvas courses, assessments, and Credit for Prior Learning (CPL) options around that content, and the courses are shared openly with all 116 colleges.

Course Design and Integration

About 189 hours across eight courses plus a job-search module. The CCC course family splits this into five 1-unit micro-credentials (Tier 1) or five 3-unit semester courses (Tier 2).

The January 2026 faculty focus group identified five packaging options:

  • Full certificate (12 units): four 3-unit courses. Financial-aid eligible. Requires curriculum approval.
  • Embedded modules: certificate content inside existing approved courses. No new approval needed.
  • Stackable micro-credentials: small units that build toward a full certificate.
  • Noncredit / continuing education: the fastest option to launch, and eligible for Strong Workforce funding.
  • Hybrid credit + industry certificate: students earn both credentials at once.

Faculty strongly favored hybrid packaging (88%) and embedded modules (88%) over entirely new standalone courses (25%).
