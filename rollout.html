May 2026

Statewide Kickoff

The Data Analytics pathway launched on May 11, 2026 in San Francisco. Chancellor Sonya Christian, Chancellor's Office leaders for workforce and institutional effectiveness, and the Academic Senate for California Community Colleges attended, along with more than 30 faculty from every region.

Faculty saw the course family, the SJCC model, and the CPL options, then helped plan how the regional convenings would run.

What every convening covers

  • The San Jose City College proof point
  • Google Career Certificates and the DA course family
  • Credit for Prior Learning and industry certificates
  • A faculty focus-group discussion on local adoption

Credit model example

Las Positas College's CIS 10 Business Data Analytics uses Google Data Analytics certificate content inside a credit course. Enrolled students get certificate access at no cost.

Noncredit model example

West Valley College offers a noncredit Certificate of Completion in Google Data Analytics, alongside noncredit Google IT Support, IT Automation, and Digital Marketing certificates.

National Collaboration

Microcredentials Community of Practice

The California Community Colleges belong to a national Microcredentials Community of Practice with Google, convened by the National Association of System Heads (NASH). Ithaka S+R evaluates it. Through it, CCC shares what it is learning about embedding industry certificates in credit pathways and learns from other public higher education systems.

Making It Last

Path to Permanence

The goal is to remove the approval, funding, and content-cost barriers that slow adoption at each individual college. The initiative is working toward:

  • Model curriculum endorsed by the Academic Senate
  • Openly licensed course content for all 116 colleges
  • CPL pathways recognized in the systemwide CPL framework
  • Strong Workforce Program funding for ongoing cohorts
  • Renewed industry agreements that keep content available
