What This Initiative Delivers

From Industry Content to College Credit

Industry partners supply certificate content. CCC faculty design courses around it that fit community college curriculum standards, so students earn a college transcript and an industry credential for the same work.

Faculty-Built Canvas Courses

Ready-to-import course shells with modules, rubrics, capstone guides, and instructor notes. Licensed CC BY 4.0 so any college can adopt and adapt them.

Credit for Prior Learning

Students who already hold a certificate can earn college credit through skills verification instead of retaking content. Nine CPL course variants are available.

First Pathway Available

Google Data Analytics

The Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate is the first certificate to be fully built out as CCC coursework. Data analytics faculty from across California shaped the design in a two-day focus group in January 2026. The result is a two-tier course family:

  • Tier 1: five 1-unit micro-credential courses (DA-101 to DA-105)
  • Tier 2: five 3-unit semester courses (DA-201 to DA-205)
  • CPL: nine assessment-only variants for certificate holders
  • Pathways: five stackable routes to an 11–15 unit certificate

A Repeatable Model

How a Certificate Becomes a CCC Pathway

Each new certificate follows the same faculty-led process developed for Data Analytics.

  1. Faculty focus group

    Discipline faculty review the certificate and recommend how to package it.

  2. Course design

    Faculty build Canvas courses, assessments, and CPL variants.

  3. Open publication

    Courses are released under CC BY 4.0 for all 116 colleges.

  4. Statewide kickoff

    The pathway is launched with system leadership and faculty.

  5. Regional convenings

    Colleges in each region plan adoption with peers and partners.

  6. Showcase & iterate

    Results are shared and courses are revised from faculty feedback.

Proof Point

The San Jose City College Model

The statewide course family builds on San Jose City College's Business and Data Analytics Certificate of Achievement. SJCC has taught Google Data Analytics content inside credit courses for several years. The approach grew out of a Bay Area pilot with Stanford Digital Education and the Bay Area Community College Consortium.

SJCC's hybrid courses pair certificate modules with faculty-led instruction and projects. Completion in the first two courses has averaged about 70%, and the 8-week accelerated format has worked best.
